Save your work before an update and keep a copy of important projects.

Saving and copying

  • Tap SAVE to save the project. Hold SAVE and press a pattern key to copy the current pattern there.
  • Hold SAVE and turn PRESETS to save a sound or drum kit. Hold SAVE and press OCT− to return the track sound to its saved state.
  • In the browser groovebox, your work stays in that browser. Firmware 0.4 reads projects and user presets from 0.2 and 0.3.

Updating an FM-1

Tap SAVE first. When GhettoMachine or another compatible firmware is already installed, the update goes over USB-MIDI and restarts the unit. Updating uses a different procedure than installing over factory firmware.
