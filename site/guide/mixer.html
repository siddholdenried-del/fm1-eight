Set the balance of your tracks first, then shape the whole mix at the master bus.

Mixer and sends

Use the mixer for track levels, pan and sends. The shared effects are a reverb and tempo delay. Press FX to open the effects pages.

Master bus

Hold SEL and press GLO to jump straight to the master bus. The chain has EQ, compression, tape saturation, stereo width and a limiter, plus presets and a loudness meter.
