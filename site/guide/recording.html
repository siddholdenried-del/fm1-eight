Play a take live, enter notes while stopped, or record a knob move.

Record notes and knob moves

  • Tap REC to arm. Play while the sequencer is running and notes go to the nearest step. Turn a knob while recording and playing to record motion.
  • On an instrument’s KEYS page while stopped, playing a note or chord puts it at the cursor. Let go to move to the next step; use SELECT to leave a rest.
  • EDIT + OCT− / OCT+ undo and redo, up to eight levels.

Count-in and timing

Hold REC. On that page, K1 controls the metronome, K2 the count-in, K3 quantize and K4 the click volume. OCT− quantizes notes already in the current part. On instrument tracks, OCT+ makes them legato.
