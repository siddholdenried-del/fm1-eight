Build a part from the white step keys. Each track has its own length and rate.

Drums: 909 or 808

  • ALGORITHM → DRUMS; turn PRESETS to choose the kit.
  • In SEQ, tap a black pad key to choose a drum. Tap white keys to toggle steps. Move through pages with OCT− / OCT+ or SELECT.
  • Hold SEQ and press a step to accent it. Hold a step and turn SELECT to set its chance.
  • EDIT opens the pad’s sound controls. Hold a step and turn a knob to set a value for just that step. Held step + OCT− clears its locks.

Instrument steps

  • Select T2–T4, play a note, then press SEQ. An empty step gets the last note you played.
  • Hold a step: K1 changes the note or chord, K2 velocity, K3 gate, and SELECT chance. Pad keys 1–4 choose the step page.
