Make a beat first, then use the chapter list to get straight to the control you need. The browser groovebox runs the same firmware as the FM-1.

Make your first loop

  1. Open the browser groovebox. Click Switch on and then PLAY / STOP to try the demo groove.
  2. Use ALGORITHM to pick DRUMS. Pick a 909 or 808 kit with PRESETS.
  3. Press SEQ, tap a black key to pick a drum, then tap white keys to switch its steps on or off.
  4. Turn ALGORITHM to T2, pick a sound with PRESETS, and play the keys.
  5. Tap SAVE to keep the project.

Controls you’ll use everywhere

ALGORITHM / PRESETS
Select the track / choose its kit or sound.
SELECT / K1–K4
Page or cursor / the four controls shown across the bottom of the screen.
EDIT, ENV, LFO, ARP, FX, GLO
Open the page. Press it again for the next page.
HOME
Scene grid, then SONG, then SETUP, then back to the track.
