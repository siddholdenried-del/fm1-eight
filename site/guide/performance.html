Use HOME to move from making one loop to playing whole patterns together.

Play live

Press HOME to open the scene grid. Each track can play a different pattern. A clip is just one track’s pattern; a scene sets all tracks to the same pattern.

The performance keys give you live moves like roll, fill, stutter, filter and tape stop. The screen shows track levels, voices in use and CPU load.

Build a song

From the scene grid, press HOME again to get to SONG, where you arrange the pattern chain. Press it again for SETUP, then again to get back to the track.
